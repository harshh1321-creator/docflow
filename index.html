How DocFlow Works
Five steps. Zero manual work.

Every document that arrives in your inbox goes through the same automated pipeline — classified, extracted, validated, and exported before you can open the email yourself.

01
Email & Document Intake

DocFlow polls your IMAP inbox every 5 minutes, or watches a dedicated folder in real time. PDF attachments are saved the moment they arrive — no forwarding, no portals, no action needed from your team.

IMAP · SMTP · Folder Watch · Web Upload
02
AI Classification

Before extraction begins, Claude AI reads the document and identifies its type — invoice, contract, insurance claim, or generic document. The right extraction engine fires every time, so nothing generic falls through.

Claude Vision · Multi-class · Confidence Scored
03
Field Extraction

Text-layer extraction for digital PDFs. Claude Vision for scanned and image-based documents. Each document type has its own tuned prompt — extracting every field, line item, and date with confidence scores per field.

pdfplumber · Claude Vision · Per-field confidence
04
Validation & Review Queue

Required fields checked. Amounts cross-validated. Vendors fuzzy-matched against your database. Documents scoring below 75% confidence are held for human review — nothing reaches your accounting system until it's clean.

Rule engine · Fuzzy match · Human-in-the-loop
05
Output & Export

Clean, structured data lands exactly where you need it — Google Sheets, QuickBooks Online, Xero, or a custom webhook. Enable multiple destinations simultaneously. One extraction, many outputs.

Google Sheets · QuickBooks · Xero · Webhook

Document Intelligence
Built to read what your business runs on

Four document categories, each with its own dedicated extraction engine, validation ruleset, and output schema. No template setup. No per-vendor configuration.

Invoices & Bills

Extracts every field from any vendor invoice — digital or scanned. Vendor matching handles name variations like "ACME Corp" vs "ACME CORPORATION LTD" automatically.

vendor name invoice # line items due date payment terms tax total amount
Contracts & Agreements

Surfaces the structured data inside any contract — parties, dates, values, and key clauses — without you having to read a word. Expiry tracking built in.

party A & B effective date expiry date contract value key clauses governing law
Insurance Claims & Policies

Captures every field from claims submissions — claimant details, policy numbers, incident dates, and coverage information — with a full audit trail on every document processed.

claimant name policy # incident date claim amount coverage type claim status adjuster
Generic Documents & Forms

For everything else — purchase orders, delivery notes, regulatory forms, or any structured document type. DocFlow extracts key fields and a one-line summary automatically.

document title parties identified key fields one-line summary dates
